Normal distribution
$$\mathcal{N}_{\mu, \sigma} (x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} \exp\Big( - \frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\Big)$$

CDF of normal distribution (term $1/2$ because erf integration starts from $0$)
$$\mathcal{C}_{\mu, \sigma} (x) = \frac{1}{2}\Big[ 1+ \text{erf}\Big( \frac{x-\mu}{\sqrt{2}\sigma}\Big)\Big]$$

\paragraph{Finding $n_\sigma$ from $\Delta x$}
Extract probability for minimal one-sided deviation $\Delta x  =x-\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$ from mean of normal distribution 
$$p = \text{erf}\Big( \frac{\Delta x}{\sqrt{2}\sigma}\Big)$$

Rewrite a probability $p$ (close to $1$ = large $n_\sigma$) into number of sigmas $n_\sigma$
$$n_\sigma = \sqrt{2} \text{erfinv} (p)$$

\paragraph{Finding $\Delta x$ from $n_\sigma$}
Just invert equations from above
$$p = \text{erf}\Big( \frac{n_\sigma}{\sqrt{2}}\Big), \qquad \Delta x = \sqrt{2}\sigma \text{erfinv}(p)$$
